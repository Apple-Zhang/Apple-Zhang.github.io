SVM-Intro is an annual offline course on SVM fundamentals, offered since 2020. It includes multi-version (2020–2025) lecture notes, MATLAB and Python implementations, and recorded lectures. Beginner-friendly.

Course website: [github.com/Apple-Zhang/SVM-Intro](https://github.com/Apple-Zhang/SVM-Intro).
