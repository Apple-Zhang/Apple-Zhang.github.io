Joker is a general nonlinear machine learning framework for large-scale data, built on joint optimization of lightweight kernel machines. It processes **10 million data points on a single consumer-grade GPU with 10 GB memory**.

[Joker: Joint Optimization Framework for Lightweight Kernel Machines](https://github.com/Apple-Zhang/Joker-paper) (ICML 2025, CCF-A).
