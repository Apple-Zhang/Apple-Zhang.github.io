I am a **visiting research student** at the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.hkbu.edu.hk/), Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), and a **PhD candidate** in the School of Computing and Software, [Shenzhen University](https://www.szu.edu.cn/en/), China.

My research lies at the intersection of **optimization**, **large-scale learning**, and **representation learning**. I focus on scalable classification, feature extraction, and computationally efficient optimization algorithms — designing methods that can train on million-scale datasets under limited memory and consumer-grade hardware, with rigorous theoretical analysis and engineering-efficient implementations.

I have published 14 academic papers, including five as first author in top venues such as *ICML*, *IEEE TPAMI*, *IEEE TNNLS*, *IEEE TCYB*, and *Pattern Recognition*, with additional work on generalization theory of graph neural networks and medical image segmentation. My representative work includes [Joker](https://github.com/Apple-Zhang/Joker-paper), a joint optimization framework for lightweight kernel machines that processes 10 million data points on a 10 GB consumer GPU, and [LighTwSVM](https://github.com/Apple-Zhang/LighTwSVM-paper), a linear nonparallel classifier that is about 2× faster than LIBLINEAR with roughly 50% lower memory usage.
